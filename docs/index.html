The Mandate

Operation at the intersection of institutional finance and proprietary agentic technology.

Family offices pursuing direct acquisitions face a structural problem. They have capital and conviction but limited internal capacity to source, qualify and execute off-market transactions at pace. I operate as a fractional deal principal for selective family offices and private capital groups, providing direct origination capacity, acquisition process leadership and dealmaker representation from first target to signed term sheet.

How I Work

Proprietary agentic workflows and GTM-engineered outreach identify off-market acquisition targets across Nordic and European mid-markets that never reach traditional intermediaries. Direct management of the full acquisition process including seller dialogue, commercial assessment, LOI structuring and due diligence coordination. Principal-to-principal engagement throughout, applying underwriting discipline developed across 20+ structured acquisition processes. Strictly selective. Maximum two to three active mandates simultaneously.
